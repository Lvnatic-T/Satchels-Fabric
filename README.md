This is a Fabric fork of [Vercte's Satchels](https://modrinth.com/mod/vercte-satchels) mod, bringing six additional inventory slots and a swappable hotbar to **your** game! Whether you're working on a massive build, exploring new terrain, or simply trying to organize your inventory, a Satchel gives you some extra breathing room without requiring a separate backpack interface.

# What does Satchels offer you?

### Additional Inventory Slots & Accessibility:
Expand your inventory with six additional slots, accessible through your inventory & containers

# Controls

| Controls | Action                                                                                       |
|---|----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| `V` | Toggle between your regular hotbar and Satchel hotbar                                        |
| `Shift + Hotbar Key` | Swap an inventory item with the corresponding Satchel slot while an inventory screen is open |
| Controls → Satchel Position | Change the position of the Satchel slots, can also be configured in the config instead       |

The hotbar toggle can be rebound in Minecraft's Controls menu      
Shift-swapping can be disabled through the client configuration
